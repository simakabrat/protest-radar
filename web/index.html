How alerting works. An event is CONFIRMED only when it scores at or above the confirm threshold, uses forward-looking language, is dated in the future or published within the last 7 days, and matches a US location. That combination — and only that — fires the burst of urgent messages. REPORTED means a real protest that already happened, is outside the US, or could not be dated; it appears here but never texts you.
